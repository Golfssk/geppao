# GepPao Business Model v2 — Media + Utility

Status: operating hypothesis for the Technical MVP and audience-building stage  
Updated: 27 September 2026

## 1. Strategic position

GepPao is a travel-media brand connected to a Local Trip Intelligence product.

- **GepPao Media** creates useful travel stories, destination guides, news and local discovery.
- **เก็บเป๋า ไปไหน?** organizes destinations and turns editorial interest into an actionable trip.
- **Planner** converts intent into a saved, editable and shareable itinerary using published Local Data.
- **Owner tools** keep operational facts accurate and remain free while owner adoption is unproven.

The system is not a generic blog and not an OTA at this stage. Its advantage is the connection between editorial context, structured local data and trip planning.

## 2. Growth loop

1. Search, social and newsletter bring readers to a story.
2. Stories connect to a Destination, Place, Event or Trip idea.
3. Readers continue to **เก็บเป๋า ไปไหน?** or Planner.
4. Planner creates measurable intent: saved trip, map open, contact action or outbound booking click.
5. Intent data identifies useful content and commercial opportunities.
6. Owner updates improve Local Data and Planner quality.
7. Better utility creates stronger stories, retention and repeat discovery.

## 3. Audience products

### Free editorial

- Destination guides
- Itineraries and road trips
- Food, cafés, accommodation and activities
- Events and timely travel news
- Family, pet, couple, budget and accessibility guides
- Local stories and owner interviews
- Practical travel advice

Editorial facts must be source-backed. Commercial content must be labeled. AI may assist drafting but cannot invent place facts, prices, hours, coordinates, contacts or rights.

### Free utility

- Destination discovery
- Place and Event search
- Planner
- Saved/shared trips
- Map and contact actions

## 4. Revenue layers

### Layer 1 — Affiliate revenue

Test first because it does not require an owner subscription product.

Candidate categories:

- Accommodation
- Activities and attraction tickets
- Transport and car rental
- Travel insurance
- eSIM and connectivity

Every affiliate relationship must be disclosed. Affiliate status cannot change organic Planner ranking.

### Layer 2 — Advertising

- Programmatic display advertising after meaningful traffic exists
- Direct-sold placements in predefined slots
- Destination or newsletter sponsorship

Ads must not block core reading or planning, create layout shift, or imitate an organic recommendation.

### Layer 3 — Sponsored editorial

- Sponsored story
- New-opening feature
- Seasonal destination campaign
- Branded guide or video

Requirements:

- Clear `Sponsored / เนื้อหาสนับสนุน` label
- Named sponsor
- Separate reporting from organic reach
- Editorial review and media-rights evidence
- No hidden influence on Planner scoring

### Layer 4 — Referral and qualified leads

Start only when GepPao can attribute a traveler action and the business explicitly opts in.

Examples:

- Booking referral
- Request for quotation
- Contact or LINE lead
- Activity or event interest

### Layer 5 — Digital products and membership

Optional experiments after repeat readership:

- Downloadable trip packs
- Premium curated itinerary
- Member-only newsletter or trip ideas
- Ad-light membership

### Layer 6 — Owner Operations Pro

Preserve the original hypothesis for a later workflow-validation stage:

- Multi-location and team permissions
- Bulk and scheduled updates
- Expiry reminders
- Lead workflow
- Advanced demand analytics
- Export

Free listing, fact correction and organic relevance remain free.

## 5. Commercial safeguards

GepPao will not:

- Sell hidden ranking
- Charge an owner to correct core facts
- Auto-publish imported content
- Use unlicensed media
- Resell personal data
- Publish fake reviews or AI-generated venue claims
- Promise traffic or bookings without evidence

## 6. Stage gates

### Stage M1 — Media foundation (current)

Build Article CMS, Destination Hub, SEO foundation, disclosures and article-to-Planner paths.

Success signals:

- Published source-backed stories
- Organic impressions and engaged reading
- Story-to-Destination and Story-to-Planner clicks
- Newsletter or social return traffic

No paid Owner plan.

### Stage M2 — Audience validation

Test repeatable editorial formats and distribution.

Decision metrics:

- Organic landing sessions
- Engaged reading rate
- Returning-reader rate
- Destination-to-Planner conversion
- Planner-to-saved-trip conversion
- Outbound commercial clicks

Small Affiliate and clearly labeled Sponsored experiments are allowed.

### Stage M3 — Demand monetization

Begin only when traffic produces repeatable, attributable intent.

- Affiliate optimization
- Qualified lead pilots
- Direct sponsorship packages
- Manual campaign reporting

Do not build a full booking engine yet.

### Stage M4 — Owner workflow monetization

Begin only after active owners repeatedly maintain information and a small cohort agrees to pay for an operational outcome.

Run a concierge pilot before billing development.

## 7. Destination rollout

1. Khao Yai / Pak Chong — content + Local Data + Planner
2. Pattaya — content hub first
3. Kanchanaburi — content hub first
4. Nakhon Nayok — content hub first

A destination receives Planner support only when its Place/Event coverage and operational data pass readiness checks. Publishing editorial content alone does not make a destination Planner-ready.

## 8. Measurement additions

Add these events before monetization experiments:

- `article_view`
- `story_to_destination`
- `story_to_planner`
- `destination_view`
- `affiliate_click`
- `sponsored_content_view`
- `newsletter_signup`

Never store raw search text, article-form drafts or personal contact data in product analytics metadata.

## 9. Current decisions

- Homepage becomes editorial-first.
- **เก็บเป๋า ไปไหน?** becomes the Destination/Product Hub.
- Planner remains relevance-first and independent from payment.
- Affiliate + advertising + sponsored editorial are the earliest revenue candidates.
- Owner Pro, referral contracts and transactions remain later-stage hypotheses.
- Channel Manager remains a separate product.
- Pricing remains undecided until traffic, intent and willingness-to-pay evidence exist.
