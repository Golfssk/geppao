# GepPao Legal Launch Review — Draft for Counsel

**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** Operational review completed; not legal advice; public launch approval remains pending.

## Decision

Current Privacy, Terms, Support and Owner Import Consent pages are **launch drafts**, not final legal text. They cover the product intent but omit information that materially affects transparency and enforceability.

## Privacy Notice — required additions

1. Identify the legal data controller: full registered name, address, registration/tax details where applicable, and privacy contact.
2. Add a table by data category: account, contact inquiry, owner/business submission, trip data, analytics metadata, security logs and imported-source provenance.
3. State purpose and lawful basis for each category; do not use consent when another lawful basis is actually relied upon.
4. Name recipient categories/processors and explain international transfers involving Supabase, Vercel, Google/GitHub OAuth or analytics providers.
5. Set operational retention periods and deletion/anonymization triggers rather than saying only “as necessary.”
6. Add all applicable data-subject rights, identity verification, response channel and complaint route.
7. Explain essential cookies/session storage and any analytics storage.
8. Add children/minor handling and owner responsibility where the service is not directed to children.
9. Add policy-change/version/effective-date mechanics.
10. Document a data-breach and incident-response process internally.

Thai PDPC publishes official privacy-notice materials.[^https://www.pdpc.or.th/privacy] The GPPC example demonstrates retention and data-subject-rights sections.[^https://gppc.pdpc.or.th/privacy-policy/] Cross-border transfers should be reviewed against the PDPC’s published Section 28 criteria.[^https://www.pdpc.or.th/en/22822]

## Terms — required additions

- operator identity and acceptance/effective date;
- account eligibility and account security;
- acceptable use and prohibited scraping/abuse;
- owner warranties that submitted facts and media are accurate, lawful and licensed;
- license granted to GepPao for submitted text/media, with scope and termination consequences;
- moderation, suspension, correction and takedown rights;
- planner disclaimer: estimated route/time/cost; users must verify operational facts before travel;
- sponsored-content labeling and separation from organic ranking;
- third-party links/bookings disclaimers;
- limitation of liability and indemnity language reviewed for Thai enforceability;
- governing law, venue and dispute handling;
- termination/data export/deletion consequences.

## Support — required additions

- published support channel and response targets;
- incident categories and escalation path;
- privacy-rights request path separated from ordinary support;
- content correction and media takedown route;
- emergency disclaimer: GepPao is not an emergency service;
- internal owner, severity matrix and incident log.

## Owner Import Consent — required additions

The owner must positively confirm that:

- they control the business or are authorized to act;
- all submitted facts are accurate to the best of their knowledge;
- each image has an identified rights holder and permission/license basis;
- GepPao may store, resize, display and distribute approved media for service operation and promotion;
- source URLs/provenance may be retained for audit;
- imported content stays Draft until review;
- permission can be withdrawn subject to records required for legal/audit purposes;
- takedown/correction requests can be submitted through the stated channel.

Store the consent version, timestamp, user ID, business ID and evidence reference. Do not rely on a single generic checkbox for all rights.

## Launch status

**NO-GO for full public/commercial launch** until the above decisions are completed and approved by qualified Thai counsel. Internal testing may continue with minimal personal data, Draft-only owner submissions and documented access controls.
