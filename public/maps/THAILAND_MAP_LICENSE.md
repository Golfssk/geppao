GepPao modifications: simplified linework (0.018-degree Douglas–Peucker tolerance), small-island omission for display, projected to a local SVG coordinate system; province-interior display markers and bounds derived from the source. This is an illustrative province-selection map, not an authoritative legal boundary or navigation product. Markers are NOT landmark coordinates.

The derived geographic database is offered under ODbL-1.0. Download the complete derived database: /maps/thailand-provinces.json . Retain the attribution and license when redistributing it. Editorial landmark suggestions in the separate province-content file are not geographic boundary data. No claim is made that these suggestions are verified public Place records or that every province has published catalog results.
